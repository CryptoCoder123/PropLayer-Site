03 / BEFORE YOU START

System requirements

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit (x64)
  • About 400 MB of free disk space
  • An internet connection to sign in and check your subscription
  • A Prop Layer account with an active subscription to run the overlay

04 / INSTALLING

Four steps

  1. Run the installer you just downloaded.
  2. The app is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen shows “Windows protected your PC”. Choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Open Prop Layer and sign in with your Prop Layer account.
  4. Start your stream and press Ctrl + Shift + P to show the overlay.
