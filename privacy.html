Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

Prop Layer ("we", "our", "us") is a desktop application that overlays real-time player stats and prop odds on sports broadcasts. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

2. Data We Collect

Prop Layer is designed with privacy in mind. Specifically:

  • No video data is sent to our servers. All computer vision inference runs locally on your machine.
  • API keys stay local. Your Anthropic Claude API key and The Odds API key are stored only on your device and are never transmitted to Prop Layer servers.
  • Waitlist email. If you join our waitlist, we collect your email address solely to notify you about product updates and early access.

3. Third-Party Services

Prop Layer integrates with third-party APIs at your direction:

  • Anthropic Claude API — used for jersey identification. Subject to Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • The Odds API — used for live prop odds. Subject to their own privacy policy.

4. Data Retention

We retain waitlist email addresses until you unsubscribe or request deletion. We do not sell or share your email with third parties.

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at Help@prop-layer.com.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify waitlist subscribers of material changes via email.

7. Contact

Questions? Email us at Help@prop-layer.com.

Website forms, analytics and interactive demo

Website additions updated October 2026. Early-access requests may include your email, sport preference, device and request for a recorded demo. Partnership inquiries may include your email, name, organization, area of interest and message. We use these details to respond to your request and provide the updates described on the form.

Forms are processed by Formspree. This website uses Google Analytics to understand site use and successful form submissions. Names, email addresses and form messages are not included in the custom submission event.

The interactive website demo uses fictional players and sample artwork. Your scene selections stay within the current page and are not saved to browser storage or sent to a product account. Older demo links may include a fictional player selection and image view; these contain no form information.

Accounts and payments

Account additions updated October 2026. Creating a Prop Layer account stores your email address and a password, which is hashed by our authentication provider — we never see or store the password itself. If you subscribe, we store the identifiers and status of your subscription: our provider’s customer and subscription identifiers, the plan, the subscription status, the current period end, whether a cancellation is scheduled, and the related timestamps. We do not store or receive your card number, which stays with our payment processor.

The processors involved are:

  • Supabase — hosts the account system, the subscription records and the server code that answers “is this subscription active?”. Subject to Supabase’s Privacy Policy.
  • Stripe — takes payment, stores your card details, sends receipts and runs the billing portal where you cancel or update your card. Subject to Stripe’s Privacy Policy.
  • Our email provider — delivers confirmation and password-reset messages.
  • GitHub — hosts this website and the application downloads. Subject to GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

The Prop Layer desktop application contacts the account service to sign you in and to check whether your subscription is active. Those requests send only your account token and the application version. The application never sends your screen contents, the video you are watching, or any image from your display to us or to any processor as part of signing in or checking your subscription.

To delete your account and the data described here, email Help@prop-layer.com. Deleting your account removes your subscription records; records our payment processor must keep for tax and accounting purposes remain with them for as long as the law requires.
