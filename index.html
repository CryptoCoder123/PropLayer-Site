03 / GOOD QUESTIONS

A little more
context.

Is the interactive panel the actual app?+

This is an interactive website recreation of the Prop Layer panel. Choose basketball, football, baseball or hockey, then a sample scene or player; the panel loads the corresponding original image and badge values. The athletes and values are fictional, and the badges are already part of the artwork. The working desktop app places player-information badges over a sports broadcast; this demo does not run the app or connect to live data.

What can I use today?+

Basketball (NBA), football (NFL), baseball (MLB) and hockey (NHL) are working in Prop Layer’s Windows alpha. You can request early access or a recorded alpha demo using the form below. Access is coordinated by the team; submitting the form does not immediately unlock a download.

What do I need to run Prop Layer?+

The current alpha is for Windows. Depending on the alpha setup, third-party API keys may be required for player identification and odds. Setup, compatibility and any third-party costs should be confirmed with the team before you begin.

How much does it cost?+

Pricing has not been announced. Joining the early-access list is free and does not require payment details. Product pricing and availability will be shared as plans are finalized.

Can I place a bet through Prop Layer?+

Prop Layer is an information overlay, not a sportsbook. The website demo contains sample information only and does not accept wagers or provide live betting lines.
