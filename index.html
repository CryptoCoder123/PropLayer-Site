The Process

From broadcast to insight in seconds

PropVision runs silently in the background while you watch. Zero setup required after install.

1

Watch Any Broadcast

Open your stream — YouTube, ESPN app, cable TV, whatever you use. PropVision's always-on-top overlay sits transparently above without touching your source.

2

AI Detects & Identifies

YOLOv8 detects players in real time. ByteTrack keeps consistent identities across frames. Claude AI reads jersey numbers and resolves player identity with high confidence.

3

Stay In The Game

Stats and prop insights appear directly over each player — so you never have to switch apps, tabs, or devices to understand what matters in real time.

Features

Built for real-time betting decisions

Every feature designed around one goal: getting you the right data at the right moment.

🎯

Real-Time Player Detection

YOLOv8 + ByteTrack delivers consistent player tracking at broadcast frame rates with sub-50ms detection latency.

🤖

AI Jersey Identification

Claude AI reads jersey numbers from video frames and resolves them against team rosters — no manual tagging needed. Uses your own Anthropic API key.

📊

Live Prop Odds

The Odds API integration pulls real-time lines from major sportsbooks, displayed directly on each player's badge. Uses your own Odds API key.

🪟

True Overlay — No Interference

Always-on-top transparent Electron window. Doesn't modify your stream, inject into browsers, or require any special permissions.

📡

Works Over Any Source

YouTube TV, ESPN+, League Pass, cable box capture — if it's on your screen, PropVision can overlay it.

🏀

Season Stats on Badge

Points, rebounds, assists, blocks, 3PM — per-game season averages pulled live and surfaced exactly when you need them.

📱

No Second Screen

See player stats and prop insights directly on the game instead of checking a separate app or device.

🏈
